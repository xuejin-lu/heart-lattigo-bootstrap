The observed overhead is **combined instrumentation** (many clock calls/selected branches/event collection), not specifically clock-only, and is not a measured Bootstrap speedup. Task006's original 539-event trace and Task005 timings must remain immutable.

IMPORTANT fixture-realism observation from Task004's committed 212-line test patch: the synthetic `task004Fixture` sets component c1 to the NEGATIVE of c0, not zero. Current Fast Bootstrap public zero-secret simulation **requires c1=0 at its input boundary** (`circuits/ckks/bootstrapping/fast_bootstrap.go`, `validateFastBootstrapPublicInputs`). Whether the accepted EvalMod stages retain zero c1 must be demonstrated from operation source or suitable maintained regression tests; do not assume that the boundary invariant proves every intermediate value. Do not claim the synthetic nonzero-c1 fixture is a representation of actual Fast E32 EvalMod distributions. A zero-secret simulation is not cryptographically secure FHE.

Goal: decide whether the Q0123 per-coefficient Rescale kernel deserves an actual performance improvement, using realistic component-state controls, then attempt **at most one** bit-exact, small, reversible local arithmetic implementation candidate in an isolated copy, measured uninstrumented.
